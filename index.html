Saurabh Mehta

The mind behind the magic

Saurabh Mehta

Music ProducerComposerSongwriter Sound EngineerVoice ArtistAd Film Maker Creativity Coach

Saurabh Mehta is the founder of Studio10a and Vocast Media, with more than twenty years spent making brands and stories sound the way they should. Sound engineer, music producer, voice artist, ad film maker and creativity coach - the range is deliberate, because a campaign rarely needs only one of those.

His compositions have been carried by some of India's most recognised voices: Shiv Tandav Stotra with Ashutosh Rana, and Dashanan and Ramleela with Kailash Kher and Sonu Nigam for the magnum opus play Humare Ram. Over the years he has worked alongside Amitabh Bachchan, Shankar Mahadevan, Annu Kapoor, Anup Jalota, Rochak Kohli, Nitish Bharadwaj, Neelesh Misra, Malini Awasthi, Shruti Haasan and Aalok Shrivastav.

Coming up through the production house side of the business, his work has run through most of the country's leading agencies - Ogilvy, Grey Worldwide, Leo Burnett, J. Walter Thompson, Lowe Lintas, McCann, Dentsu, Wunderman Thompson, DDB Mudra, Euro RSCG and Blue Mango - for national brands including T-Series, Dabur, Mankind Pharma, Meesho, Pocket FM, Prega News, Manforce, Lupin and Gas-O-Fast. His sound design and direction on the radio show Dharohar with Ashutosh Rana won Gold at the New York Festivals Radio Awards.

Two decades in radio and creative production left him with one instinct he still applies to every brief:

“Is there a simpler, more creative, more elegant way to achieve this?”

That question is what Studio10a is built on. He is currently producing music for upcoming feature films and web series, and building the studio into the partner brands call when the idea matters as much as the execution.

Why “Studio10a”?

It all began in Room 10A

Curious about the origins of Studio10a's name? It all began back in the early 2000s in Chennai. Picture Saurabh and his four fellow sound-engineering students - Gopinath, Navin, Gautam D, and Ankur - navigating life with big dreams and small wallets. Amidst the sea of room numbers in their hostel, one room stood out - ROOM 10A

Inside Room 10A there was a single computer, owned by Saurabh's friend Gopinath. That computer became the epicenter of their universe, where Saurabh and his buddies experimented with sound engineering. Late nights, passion, and a shared computer gave rise to a bond that would last a lifetime.

Years later, Saurabh turned the legacy of Room 10A into Studio10a Productions. With dedication and a spark of creativity, he transformed a small room into a hub of sound and innovation.

Today, Studio10a Productions stands as a tribute to Gopinath, Navin, Gautam D, and Ankur - friends who shaped its origins, each now carving their own path. Studio10a is a gathering of innovative thinkers, passionate creatives, and media professionals, all driven by the same spirit that once echoed in Room 10A.

From that single computer to a thriving space - welcome to Studio10a, where the story of Room 10A became a journey of friendship, innovation, and creative camaraderie.
