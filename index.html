Why “Studio10a”?

It all began in Room 10A

Curious about the origins of Studio10a's name? It all began back in the early 2000s in Chennai. Picture Saurabh and his four fellow sound-engineering students - Gopinath, Navin, Gautam D, and Ankur - navigating life with big dreams and small wallets. Amidst the sea of room numbers in their hostel, one room stood out - ROOM 10A

Inside Room 10A there was a single computer, owned by Saurabh's friend Gopinath. That computer became the epicenter of their universe, where Saurabh and his buddies experimented with sound engineering. Late nights, passion, and a shared computer gave rise to a bond that would last a lifetime.

Years later, Saurabh turned the legacy of Room 10A into Studio10a Productions. With dedication and a spark of creativity, he transformed a small room into a hub of sound and innovation.

Today, Studio10a Productions stands as a tribute to Gopinath, Navin, Gautam D, and Ankur - friends who shaped its origins, each now carving their own path. Studio10a is a gathering of innovative thinkers, passionate creatives, and media professionals, all driven by the same spirit that once echoed in Room 10A.

From that single computer to a thriving space - welcome to Studio10a, where the story of Room 10A became a journey of friendship, innovation, and creative camaraderie.
