02 The idea

One click saved is one click earned.

You make music, not menus. ReaStudio takes a little effort out of the jobs you repeat all day: picking a tool, finding a setting, grouping a folder.

Fewer clicks. Fewer steps. Less hunting. Over a session, that is time you get to keep.

03 Who it is for

Two DAWs. One set of habits.

For anyone who works in two DAWs. ReaStudio makes REAPER open the way you already expect, so changing rooms costs no thought.

Keep the DAW you like. Borrow what the other one does better. Stop re-learning where things are.

04 One window

Everything exactly where you reach for it.

Tool row on top. Track list and inspector on the left. Your tracks in the middle. Transport at the bottom. Nothing to go looking for.

05 The tool row

Pick a tool. Click a clip. Done.

Quantize, timebase and snap share one box. A macro bar keeps your favourite actions one click away instead of three menus deep.

  • Mute, delete, paint and stretch markers act on whatever is under the mouse.
  • Ripple, auto-crossfade, grouping and locking sit in the same row.
06 Track list and inspector

Track, clip and channel. One column, no windows.

Pick a track and everything about it is already open next to your tracks. No floating windows to chase.

  • Track: tempo, timestretch, grouping, delay, input, automation.
  • Event: the selected clip's own settings.
  • Channel: pan, mute, solo, fader, inserts and sends.

Every value is REAPER's own, read live, so the inspector always agrees with the track list and the mixer.

07 The mixer

The whole mix, in one row.

Inserts on top, sends under them, then the fader, a big icon and a colour for every channel. One row, so you read the mix left to right and never hunt for a strip.

08 Folders and groups

Group a whole folder with one click.

Every folder track gets a GROUP button. Click it and its tracks edit, mute and solo together. Click again to ungroup.

  • Folder Events puts a box over each stretch of clips, so move, split and copy act on the whole group.
  • Opt-in, and off with one click.
09 Track icons

Fifty-three icons, Indian instruments included.

Tabla, dholak, dhol, mridangam, sitar, sarangi, santoor, bansuri, shehnai, harmonium, tanpura and veena sit beside the piano, the strings, the choir and the studio mic. Find the track you want without reading a single name.

10 The small tools

The jobs you repeat, handled.

Export Stems and Mixdown

Sources, location, format and range on one page. The folder is always the open song's own.

Tempo tools

Detect the tempo of a clip, or build a tempo map from one.

Start page

A start page for opening your songs.

Ruler lanes

Named lanes above the song, such as arranger, chords and markers.

Make it yours: choose where track colour shows, how strong it is, and which transport buttons appear.

11 Built for it

Not everything is built in. Some of it is built for it.

ReaStudio sets up the room. These tools do their work inside it, one button away on the toolbar, so the job never sends you out to another app.

VoiceLab

Changes the voice of a clip right on the timeline.

VoiceLab Track

Does it on any track: drag audio in, convert, drag it back.

TextLab

Turns a typed script into speech.

Studio10A Stream

Your client hears the session live in a browser, with nothing to install, and you can talk to each other. One toolbar button starts it, another opens your talkback mic.

Voice Suite is a separate free download. See Voice Suite. Studio10A Stream runs in our own sessions today and is on its way to the build.
