The three plugins

Convert a clip, a track, or a script

VoiceLab changes the voice of a clip right on the timeline (ARA2). VoiceLab Track does it on any track in any DAW: drag audio in, convert, drag it back. TextLab turns a typed script into speech. No exports, no browser tabs, no copy-paste.

Three changes that take a little effort out of work you already do all day.

01 - TEXTLAB

Eleven v4 is the default, with a Turbo beside it

The most expressive ElevenLabs model is where TextLab starts now, with v4 Turbo next to it when speed matters more. Drop a [pause] or [long pause] chip into the script and the take breathes where you want it. Up to 10,000 characters in one go.

Pause chips lead the row, Eleven v4 is already chosen

02 - EVERY PLUGIN

A gain on every take, and it moves while it plays

Each generated take wears a GAIN badge, -24 to +12 dB. Preview, playback, HOST SYNC and drags all follow it, and it is saved with the project. Now you can move it live while the take plays and set the level by ear, with no stop and restart. The original file is never rewritten.

One badge per take - drag it, double-click for 0 dB

03 - TEXTLAB, NEW

Voice trim: set it once, every take comes out ready

Every voice name in TextLab now has a little dB pill. Set a hot voice to -2 dB once and every new take from it comes out trimmed, ready to drag into the session. It is one level you stop fixing by hand, and one voice that stops clipping your mix.

The pill after the name is the voice trim

Comp your takes the way a DAW does

VoiceLab Track's whole clip side was rebuilt. Your captured clips lie along a timeline, and every alternative take sits in a lane directly under the clip it came from. A clip tried in five voices is a column five deep.

One colour per voice, held across every clip - click a block and that clip plays that take

NEW

A take per clip, and it stays

Clip one on one voice, clip two on another, clip three on a third - each keeps its own choice. HOST SYNC plays all of them where they belong, so what you hear is what you see lit. Saved with the project, so it is still there tomorrow.

NEW

Jump the cursor, get separate clips

Arm TRANSFER, play, and jump the playhead between the takes you care about. Each jump starts a new clip, stamped where you landed. One pass over three takes gives you three clips in the right places instead of one long run with the gaps baked in. Logic locates differently from Studio One, and 1.6.1 reads both.

NEW

Remove a take, and change your mind

Right-click any clip or take in the lanes to take it off, with the audio left where it lives on disk. UNDO on the button row, or Cmd+Z, puts back the last removal, Clear, trim or take choice, twelve steps deep, and tells you what is coming back before you press it.

NEW

Drag it out, scroll and zoom

Takes, clips and rows in the list all drag straight onto your timeline, and Shift gathers several at once. The lane timeline slides and zooms from its own scrollbar: drag the middle to move, either end to zoom, double-click to fit.

What it does

Made by a producer, for producers

01

VoiceLab - voice to voice

Put it on a clip, pick a voice, press Convert. The clip plays in the new voice - the original untouched, one click away. Every conversion becomes a VERSION chip: A/B instantly, keep them all in the project.

02

VoiceLab Track - any track, any DAW

A normal insert plugin: drop audio in, or arm TRANSFER and simply play - it records what flows through the track, timeline-stamped, splitting into separate clips wherever you jump the cursor. Every take lands in a lane under its own clip, colour-coded by voice, one drag from your timeline. Logic, GarageBand, Ableton, anything.

03

TextLab - text to speech

Type or paste a script - Hindi, Tamil, Bangla, English, 90+ languages on Eleven v4. On a clip, the clip becomes the speech. On a track, generate and drag. Audio tags, multiple takes per line, per-line batching. ENHANCE places the tags for you - on your own free Gemini or Groq key, so it costs nothing.

04

Your whole voice library

Search, folders, favourites and free auditioning across every voice on your ElevenLabs account - synced in one click, shared by all three plugins, with a live credit meter so cost is never a surprise.

05

Batch and versions

Select many clips, convert them all with one press. One to three takes per generation with reproducible seeds. Twenty versions per clip, each addressable from the keyboard.

06

Fast hands welcome

Editable shortcut for Convert and Generate. Ctrl+1-0 and Cmd+1-0 switch versions - the shortcut is printed on every chip. Drag any version, or the original, straight onto any track.

07

Clean by principle

Dark and light skins, one settings brain across every instance, files named the way a producer would name them, a real help guide and a real uninstaller. Nothing in the UI that is not needed.

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need an ElevenLabs subscription?
You need an ElevenLabs account and API key. The free tier works for trying it; real production use spends credits on your plan. The plugins show the cost before you spend.
Does VoiceLab work in my DAW?
VoiceLab's clip magic needs ARA2 - Studio One, Nuendo, REAPER, Cakewalk. VoiceLab Track and TextLab work in any VST3 or AU host, Logic Pro and GarageBand included.
Is my audio uploaded somewhere?
Conversion sends the selected clip's audio to ElevenLabs' API under your own account, and generated audio comes back to your machine. Your API key never leaves your computer and is never stored in project files.
Why is it free?
Because at Studio10a we celebrate creativity, and the best way to celebrate a tool that makes work flow is to share it. The Voice Suite earns its keep in our own sessions every day; the only real cost - your ElevenLabs credits - is yours either way. If it makes your workflow sing, buy us a coffee.
Does ENHANCE cost anything?
No. TextLab's ENHANCE (Eleven v4 and v3) asks an AI model to place the emotion tags, and it runs on your own key - Google Gemini and Groq give one away free, no card. The plugin opens the key page for you, remembers the key, picks the model itself, and if one provider is busy it switches to another key you saved. Your words are never changed - only tags are added.
Hindi? Tamil? Bangla?
Yes - typing, display and speech. Devanagari and other scripts work natively in the script box.
