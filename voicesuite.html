What it does

Made by a producer, for producers

01

VoiceLab - voice to voice

Put it on a clip, pick a voice, press Convert. The clip plays in the new voice - the original untouched, one click away. Every conversion becomes a VERSION chip: A/B instantly, keep them all in the project.

02

TextLab - text to speech

Type or paste a script - Hindi, Tamil, Bangla, English, 70+ languages. On a clip, the clip becomes the speech. On a track, generate and drag. Emotion tags, multiple takes per line, per-line batching.

03

Your whole voice library

Search, folders, favourites and free auditioning across every voice on your ElevenLabs account - synced in one click, shared by both plugins, with a live credit meter so cost is never a surprise.

04

Batch and versions

Select many clips, convert them all with one press. One to three takes per generation with reproducible seeds. Twenty versions per clip, each addressable from the keyboard.

05

Fast hands welcome

Editable shortcut for Convert and Generate. Ctrl+1-0 and Cmd+1-0 switch versions - the shortcut is printed on every chip. Drag any version, or the original, straight onto any track.

06

Clean by principle

Dark and light skins, one settings brain across every instance, files named the way a producer would name them, a real manual and a real uninstaller. Nothing in the UI that is not needed.

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need an ElevenLabs subscription?
You need an ElevenLabs account and API key. The free tier works for trying it; real production use spends credits on your plan. The plugins show the cost before you spend.
Does VoiceLab work in my DAW?
The full clip magic needs ARA2 - Studio One, Cubase, Nuendo, REAPER, Cakewalk. TextLab's generate-and-drag mode works in any VST3 host.
Is my audio uploaded somewhere?
Conversion sends the selected clip's audio to ElevenLabs' API under your own account, and generated audio comes back to your machine. Your API key never leaves your computer and is never stored in project files.
Why is it free?
Because at Studio10a we celebrate creativity, and the best way to celebrate a tool that makes work flow is to share it. The Voice Suite earns its keep in our own sessions every day; the only real cost - your ElevenLabs credits - is yours either way. If it makes your workflow sing, buy us a coffee.
Hindi? Tamil? Bangla?
Yes - typing, display and speech. Devanagari and other scripts work natively in the script box.
